# MASARAK — Milestone 1

This folder contains the complete Milestone 1 project proposal for the MASARAK mobile application.

## Milestone 1 Objective

The goal of Milestone 1 is to define the MASARAK project idea, the problem it addresses, its target users, proposed solution, core MVP features, AI use, project timeline, anticipated risks, and team agreement.

## Project Overview

MASARAK is a mobile application designed to support and organize the cooperative training journey of university students.

The application addresses the difficulty students and supervisors face when attendance, working hours, tasks, reports, evaluations, and communication are managed through separate channels and tools.

MASARAK brings these training activities into one structured platform, allowing progress, attendance, tasks, evaluations, and reports to be followed throughout the training period.

## User Roles

MASARAK supports three main user roles:

- Student
- Field Supervisor
- Academic Supervisor

Students use the application to manage and track their cooperative training activities.

Field Supervisors monitor students' attendance, assigned tasks, progress, and performance.

Academic Supervisors monitor students' training progress and verify that assigned tasks are relevant to their academic majors and meet cooperative training requirements.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed application brings the student, field supervisor, and academic supervisor into one structured training workflow.

It centralizes the student's training records and allows progress, attendance, tasks, evaluations, and reports to be followed throughout the training period.

The application also provides academic supervisors with continuous visibility into the student's progress and the relevance of assigned tasks to the academic major, allowing concerns to be identified earlier.

## Core Features (MVP Scope)

The proposed MVP includes the following features:

### Must Have

- Training Record Setup & Role-Based Access
- Attendance, Training Hours & Reports
- Task Documentation & Progress Tracking
- Task Verification & Supervisor Approval
- Task Relevance Review & Academic Alerts
- Weekly & Final Evaluations

### Should Have

- In-App Communication

### Could Have

- Training Record Export

## Anticipated Risks & Challenges

The project proposal identifies the following risks:

### 1. Limited Mobile Development Experience

Some team members may be new to Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, which may slow development. The team will use practice tasks and shared resources to build the required skills.

### 2. Team Coordination and GitHub Conflicts

Working with seven members may cause merge conflicts or duplicated work. Each feature will have a clear owner and separate branch, with changes reviewed before merging.

### 3. Project Scope and Time Constraints

The planned features may exceed the available development time. The team will prioritize Must features first and postpone lower-priority features if needed.
