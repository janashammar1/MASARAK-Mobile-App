# MASARAK | مسارك

MASARAK is a mobile application designed to manage the cooperative training journey of university students.

## About the Project

The application connects students, field supervisors, and academic supervisors in one platform to manage and follow the training process.

It helps organize:
- Attendance and training hours
- Assigned tasks and progress
- Reports and evaluations
- Task relevance
- Supervisor feedback
- Training-related communication

## Users

- Students
- Field Supervisors
- Academic Supervisors

## Main Features

- Role-based access
- Training record management
- Attendance and training hours
- Task management and progress tracking
- Supervisor task review and approval
- Academic task relevance review
- Weekly and final evaluations
- In-app communication
- Training record export

## Technology

- Kotlin
- Android
- Jetpack Compose
