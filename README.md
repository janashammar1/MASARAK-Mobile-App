# مسارك | MASARAK

## Project Overview
MASARAK is a mobile application designed to organize and manage the cooperative training journey for university students, field supervisors, and academic supervisors.

The application brings the main training activities into one structured platform, including attendance, training hours, assigned tasks, progress tracking, evaluations, reports, and supervisor feedback.

Its goal is to reduce scattered records and improve visibility, communication, and follow-up throughout the cooperative training period.

## Target Users
- University students enrolled in cooperative training
- Field supervisors at training organizations
- Academic supervisors at the university

## Core Features
- Training record setup and role-based access
- Attendance and training hours tracking
- Task documentation and progress tracking
- Task verification and supervisor approval
- Task relevance review and academic alerts
- Weekly and final evaluations
- In-app communication
- Training record export
